First, let's calculate the total number of notebooks Ben initially had.

Ben buys 5 packs of notebooks, and each pack contains 10 notebooks.

Total notebooks = Number of packs × Notebooks per pack
Total notebooks = 5 packs × 10 notebooks/pack
Total notebooks = 50 notebooks

Next, Ben gives 40 notebooks to Yara.

Now, let's subtract the notebooks given away from the total notebooks.

Remaining notebooks = Total notebooks - Notebooks given away
Remaining notebooks = 50 notebooks - 40 notebooks
Remaining notebooks = 10 notebooks
