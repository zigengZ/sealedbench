First, let's calculate the total number of pages Ava reads from both books.

For the first book:
- Ava reads 10 pages a day for 3 days.
- Pages read from the first book = 10 pages/day * 3 days = 30 pages.

For the second book:
- Ava reads 19 pages a day for 5 days.
- Pages read from the second book = 19 pages/day * 5 days = 95 pages.

Now, let's add the pages from both books:
Total pages read = Pages from first book + Pages from second book
Total pages read = 30 pages + 95 pages = 125 pages.

Ava skips 26 pages that were blank. So, we need to subtract these from the total:
Total pages read - skipped pages = 125 pages - 26 pages = 99 pages.
