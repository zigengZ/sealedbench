First, let's calculate the total number of seats Ivy initially had.

Total seats = Number of rows × Number of seats per row
Total seats = 8 rows × 12 seats/row
Total seats = 96 seats

Next, we find out how many seats were sold.

Seats sold = Total seats sold - Total seats
Seats sold = 81 seats - 96 seats
Seats sold = -7 seats

Since the number of seats sold is negative, it means 7 seats were unsold.

Each unsold seat is worth 2 dollars.

Unsold seats worth = Number of unsold seats × Value per unsold seat
Unsold seats worth = -7 seats × $2/seat
Unsold seats worth = -$14
