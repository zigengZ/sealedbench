First, let's calculate the total number of cards Chloe has after the transactions.

Chloe buys 5 packs of cards, and each pack contains 4 cards.
So, the total number of cards Chloe has initially is:
5 packs * 4 cards/pack = 20 cards

Chloe then gives 17 cards to Ben.
So, the number of cards Chloe has left is:
20 cards - 17 cards = 3 cards

Ben gives Chloe 18 more cards.
So, the total number of cards Chloe has after receiving the additional cards is:
3 cards + 18 cards = 21 cards

Each card is worth 5 dollars.
So, the total value of Chloe's cards is:
21 cards * 5 dollars/card = 105 dollars
