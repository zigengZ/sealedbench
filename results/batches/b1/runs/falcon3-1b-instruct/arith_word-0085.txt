First, let's calculate the total number of oranges Felix initially has.

Felix buys 9 packs of oranges, and each pack contains 10 oranges.

Total oranges = Number of packs × Oranges per pack
Total oranges = 9 × 10 = 90

Next, Felix gives 42 oranges to Mina.

Now, let's find out how many oranges Felix has left.

Oranges left = Total oranges - Oranges given
Oranges left = 90 - 42 = 48

So, Felix has #### 48 oranges. [end of text]
