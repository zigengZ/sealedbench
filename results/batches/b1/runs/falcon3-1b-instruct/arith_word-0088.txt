First, let's calculate the total number of stamps Pavel has.

Pavel buys 9 packs of stamps, and each pack contains 5 stamps.
So, the total number of stamps Pavel has is:
9 packs * 5 stamps/pack = 45 stamps

Next, Pavel gives 3 stamps to Felix.

Now, let's calculate the total number of stamps Pavel has after giving some to Felix.

Pavel originally had 45 stamps.
He gives away 3 stamps, so he is left with:
45 stamps - 3 stamps = 42 stamps

Each stamp is worth 9 dollars.

To find the total value of Pavel's stamps, we multiply the number of stamps by the value per stamp:
42 stamps * 9 dollars/stamp = 378 dollars
