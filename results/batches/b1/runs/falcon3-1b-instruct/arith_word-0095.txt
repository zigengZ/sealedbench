First, let's calculate the total number of magnets Dmitri initially had.

Dmitri buys 3 packs of magnets, and each pack contains 4 magnets.
So, the total number of magnets Dmitri initially had is:
3 packs * 4 magnets/pack = 12 magnets

Then, Elena gives Dmitri 17 more magnets.

Now, let's add the number of magnets Elena gave to the initial total:
12 magnets (initial) + 17 magnets (Elena's gift) = 29 magnets

Therefore, Dmitri has #### 29 magnets. [end of text]
