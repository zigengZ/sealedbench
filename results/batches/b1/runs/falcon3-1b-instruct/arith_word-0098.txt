First, calculate the total number of buttons Esme initially has:

7 packs * 11 buttons/pack = 77 buttons

Next, calculate the total number of buttons after buying 5 more packs:

77 buttons + 5 packs * 4 buttons/pack = 77 + 20 = 97 buttons

Then, Esme gives 34 buttons to Uma:

97 buttons - 34 buttons = 63 buttons

Now, the buttons are shared equally among 3 people:

63 buttons / 3 people = 21 buttons/person
