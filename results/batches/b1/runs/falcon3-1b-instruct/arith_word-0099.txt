To find the total worth of the unsold seats, we first calculate the total number of seats set out.

Number of rows = 3
Number of seats per row = 10

Total seats = 3 rows × 10 seats/row = 30 seats

Now, we subtract the number of seats sold from the total number of seats:

Sold seats = 17

Unsold seats = Total seats - Sold seats = 30 - 17 = 13 seats

Each unsold seat is worth 9 dollars. Therefore, the total worth of the unsold seats is:

Total worth = Unsold seats × Worth per unsold seat = 13 seats × 9 dollars/seat = 117 dollars
