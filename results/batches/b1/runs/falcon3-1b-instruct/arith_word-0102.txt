First, let's calculate the total number of pages Aisha reads in one day.

For the first book:
6 pages/day * 2 days = 12 pages

For the second book:
10 pages/day * 5 days = 50 pages

Total pages read in one day = 12 + 50 = 62 pages

Now, let's calculate the total number of pages Aisha reads in 3 weeks.

Since she reads 62 pages per day, in 3 weeks (21 days):
62 pages/day * 21 days = 1342 pages

Aisha skips 10 pages that were blank, so we subtract those from the total:
1342 pages - 10 pages = 1332 pages
