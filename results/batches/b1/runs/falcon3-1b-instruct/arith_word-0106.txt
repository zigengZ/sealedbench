First, let's calculate the total number of oranges Ben initially has.

Ben buys 2 packs of oranges, and each pack contains 8 oranges.

Total oranges = Number of packs × Oranges per pack
Total oranges = 2 packs × 8 oranges/pack
Total oranges = 16 oranges

Next, Ben gives 3 oranges to Aisha.

Oranges remaining = Total oranges - Oranges given
Oranges remaining = 16 oranges - 3 oranges
Oranges remaining = 13 oranges
