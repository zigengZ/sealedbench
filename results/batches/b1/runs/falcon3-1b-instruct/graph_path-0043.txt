   - If this new distance is less than the current known distance to the neighbor, update the distance and add the neighbor to the priority queue.

3. **Termination**:
   - Continue this process until the priority queue is empty.

4. **Result**:
   - The distance from Kai to Nell is the smallest distance found in the priority queue.
