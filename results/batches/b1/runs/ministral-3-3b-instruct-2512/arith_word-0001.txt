Felix first buys 8 packs of oranges, each containing 12 oranges:
8 packs × 12 oranges = 96 oranges.

Next, he buys 2 packs with 7 oranges each:
2 packs × 7 oranges = 14 oranges.

Adding these together gives a total of:
96 oranges + 14 oranges = 110 oranges.

Sharing equally among 2 people:
110 ÷ 2 = 55 oranges.

#### 55 [end of text]
