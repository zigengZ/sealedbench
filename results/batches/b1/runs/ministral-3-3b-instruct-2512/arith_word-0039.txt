Samir initially buys 8 packs of marbles, each containing 6 marbles:
8 packs × 6 marbles = 48 marbles.

Then he buys an additional 5 packs:
5 packs × 6 marbles = 30 marbles.

Total marbles bought: 48 + 30 = 78 marbles.

After giving 32 marbles to Jamal:
78 - 32 = 46 marbles.
