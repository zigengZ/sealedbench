Kira starts with 8 packs of 10 tickets each:
8 packs × 10 tickets = 80 tickets.

Then she buys 4 packs of 2 tickets each:
4 packs × 2 tickets = 8 tickets.

Total tickets now: 80 + 8 = 88 tickets.

She gives 3 tickets to Gina, so remaining tickets:
88 - 3 = 85 tickets.

Each ticket is worth $3, so total value:
85 × 3 = 255 dollars.
