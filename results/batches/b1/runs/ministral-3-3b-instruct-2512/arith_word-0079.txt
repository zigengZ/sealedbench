Esme starts with 7 packs of 6 oranges each: 7 × 6 = 42 oranges.
Then she buys 3 more packs of 7 oranges each: 3 × 7 = 21 oranges.
Total oranges before giving to Samir: 42 + 21 = 63.
After giving 58 to Samir: 63 - 58 = 5 oranges left.
Shared equally among 5 people: 5 ÷ 5 = 1 orange per person.
