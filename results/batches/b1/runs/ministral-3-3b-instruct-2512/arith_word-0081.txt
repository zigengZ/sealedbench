Nico starts with 6 packs of 12 oranges each:
6 × 12 = 72 oranges.

Then he buys 2 packs of 8 oranges each:
2 × 8 = 16 oranges.

Total oranges before giving any away:
72 + 16 = 88 oranges.

After giving 41 oranges to Xavier:
88 - 41 = 47 oranges.

Each orange is worth $7, so total value:
47 × 7 = 329 dollars.
