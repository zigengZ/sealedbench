Esme starts with 7 packs of 11 buttons: 7 × 11 = 77 buttons.
Then she buys 5 packs of 4 buttons: 5 × 4 = 20 buttons.
Total buttons before giving away: 77 + 20 = 97.
After giving 34 buttons to Uma: 97 - 34 = 63 buttons.
Sharing equally among 3 people: 63 ÷ 3 = 21 buttons per person.
