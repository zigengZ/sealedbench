First, find the total number of seats Mina sets out:

- 4 rows with 12 seats each:  
  $4 \times 12 = 48$ seats

- Then 4 more rows with 4 seats each:  
  $4 \times 4 = 16$ seats

Total seats = $48 + 16 = 64$ seats

Then, 1 seat is sold, so the number of **unsold seats** is:  
$64 - 1 = 63$ seats

Each unsold seat is worth $2, so total value is:  
$63 \times 2 = 126$ dollars
