First, find the total number of seats Esme set up.

She sets out:
- 10 rows with 7 seats each:  
  $10 \times 7 = 70$ seats

Then adds:
- 6 rows with 9 seats each:  
  $6 \times 9 = 54$ seats

Total seats = $70 + 54 = 124$ seats

Then, 114 seats are sold, so the number of unsold seats is:  
$124 - 114 = 10$ seats

Each unsold seat is worth $11, so total value of unsold seats is:  
$10 \times 11 = 110$ dollars
