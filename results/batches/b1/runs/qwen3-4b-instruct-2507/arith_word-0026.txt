First, find the total number of seats Nico set up.

Nico sets out 10 rows with 16 seats each:

$$
10 \times 16 = 160 \text{ seats}
$$

Then he adds 3 more rows with 7 seats each:

$$
3 \times 7 = 21 \text{ seats}
$$

Total seats:

$$
160 + 21 = 181 \text{ seats}
$$

Then 126 seats are sold.

So, unsold seats:

$$
181 - 126 = 55
$$

#### 55 [end of text]
