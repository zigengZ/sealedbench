First, find the total number of buttons Jamal buys:

- 9 packs × 10 buttons = 90 buttons  
- 4 packs × 6 buttons = 24 buttons  

Total buttons = 90 + 24 = 114 buttons

Then Jamal gives 66 buttons to Ava, so the remaining buttons are:

114 - 66 = 48 buttons

These 48 buttons are shared equally among 4 people:

48 ÷ 4 = 12 buttons per person
