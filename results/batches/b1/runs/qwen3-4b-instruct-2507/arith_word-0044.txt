First, find the total number of seats Yara sets out.

She has:
- 4 rows with 9 seats each:  
  $4 \times 9 = 36$ seats

Then she adds:
- 5 rows with 5 seats each:  
  $5 \times 5 = 25$ seats

Total seats = $36 + 25 = 61$

Then, 45 seats are sold.

So, unsold seats = $61 - 45 = 16$
