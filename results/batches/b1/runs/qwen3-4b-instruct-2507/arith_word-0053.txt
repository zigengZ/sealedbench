Mina sets out 5 rows with 13 seats in each row.

First, find the total number of seats:

$$
5 \times 13 = 65 \text{ seats}
$$

Then, 64 seats are sold, so the number of unsold seats is:

$$
65 - 64 = 1 \text{ seat}
$$

Each unsold seat is worth $8, so the total value of unsold seats is:

$$
1 \times 8 = 8 \text{ dollars}
$$
