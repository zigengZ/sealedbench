First, find the total number of seats Tara sets out.

She sets up:
- 3 rows with 6 seats each:  
  $3 \times 6 = 18$ seats

Then she adds:
- 3 rows with 8 seats each:  
  $3 \times 8 = 24$ seats

Total seats = $18 + 24 = 42$

Then, 6 seats are sold.

So, unsold seats = $42 - 6 = 36$
