Let's break down how many pages Yara reads in one week:

1. First book: 10 pages/day for 5 days  
   $ 10 \times 5 = 50 $ pages

2. Second book: 9 pages/day for 3 days  
   $ 9 \times 3 = 27 $ pages

3. Then she skips 50 blank pages — these **do not count** as read pages.

So, total pages **read** in one week:  
$ 50 + 27 = 77 $ pages

She repeats this pattern for **2 weeks**, so:  
$ 77 \times 2 = 154 $ pages

#### 154 [end of text]
