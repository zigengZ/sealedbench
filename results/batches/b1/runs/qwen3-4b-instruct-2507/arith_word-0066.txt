First, find the total number of seats Cara sets up.

She sets out:

Then she adds:
- 8 rows with 7 seats each:  
  $8 \times 7 = 56$ seats

Total seats = $120 + 56 = 176$

So, unsold seats = $176 - 45 = 131$
