First, find the total number of seats Xavier sets up.

He sets up:
- 7 rows with 10 seats each:  
  $7 \times 10 = 70$ seats

Then he adds:
- 7 rows with 8 seats each:  
  $7 \times 8 = 56$ seats

Total seats = $70 + 56 = 126$

Then, 105 seats are sold.

So, unsold seats = total seats - sold seats  
$126 - 105 = 21$
