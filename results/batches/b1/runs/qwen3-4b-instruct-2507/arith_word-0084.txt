Chloe buys 5 packs of cards, and each pack has 4 cards:

5 packs × 4 cards = 20 cards

So Chloe starts with 20 cards.

Then she gives 17 cards to Ben:

20 - 17 = 3 cards left with Chloe.

Then Ben gives Chloe 18 more cards:

3 + 18 = 21 cards now with Chloe.

Each card is worth $5, so:

21 cards × $5 = $105
