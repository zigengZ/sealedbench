Felix buys 8 packs of apples, and each pack has 9 apples:

8 × 9 = 72 apples

Then Felix gives 8 apples to Leo, so he has:

72 − 8 = 64 apples left

Then Leo gives Felix 11 more apples, so Felix now has:

64 + 11 = 75 apples

Each apple is worth $6, so the total value is:

75 × 6 = 450 dollars
