First, find the total number of buttons Esme buys:

- 7 packs × 11 buttons = 77 buttons  
- 5 packs × 4 buttons = 20 buttons  

Total buttons bought:  
77 + 20 = 97 buttons

Then Esme gives 34 buttons to Uma:  
97 - 34 = 63 buttons left

These 63 buttons are shared equally among 3 people:  
63 ÷ 3 = 21

Each person gets 21 buttons.
