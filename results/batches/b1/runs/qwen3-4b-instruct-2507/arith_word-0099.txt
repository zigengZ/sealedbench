Pavel sets out 3 rows with 10 seats in each row:

Then 17 seats are sold, so the number of unsold seats is:

$ 30 - 17 = 13 $ unsold seats.

Each unsold seat is worth $9, so the total value of the unsold seats is:

$ 13 \times 9 = 117 $ dollars.
