Let's break down how many pages Aisha reads each week.

**First book:**  
She reads 6 pages a day for 2 days:  
6 × 2 = 12 pages

**Second book:**  
She reads 10 pages a day for 5 days:  
10 × 5 = 50 pages

**Total pages read in one week (before skipping):**  
12 + 50 = 62 pages

**She skips 10 blank pages** — these don't count, so we subtract them:  
62 − 10 = 52 pages per week

She repeats this pattern for **3 weeks**:  
52 × 3 = 156 pages
