First, find the total number of oranges Pavel buys:

- 5 packs with 10 oranges each:  
  $5 \times 10 = 50$ oranges

- 5 packs with 9 oranges each:  
  $5 \times 9 = 45$ oranges

Total oranges before giving to Chloe:  
$50 + 45 = 95$ oranges

Pavel gives 30 oranges to Chloe:  
$95 - 30 = 65$ oranges left

These 65 oranges are shared equally among 5 people:  
$65 \div 5 = 13$

Each person gets **13** oranges.
