First, find the total number of seats Leo sets up.

Leo has:
- 3 rows with 10 seats each:  
  $3 \times 10 = 30$ seats

Then he adds:
- 8 rows with 9 seats each:  
  $8 \times 9 = 72$ seats

Total seats = $30 + 72 = 102$

Then, 68 seats are sold.

So, unsold seats = total seats - sold seats  
$102 - 68 = 34$

#### 34 [end of text]
