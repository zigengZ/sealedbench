First, find the total number of seats Nico sets out.

Nico has:
- 7 rows with 14 seats each:  
  $7 \times 14 = 98$ seats

Then he adds:
- 4 rows with 3 seats each:  
  $4 \times 3 = 12$ seats

Total seats = $98 + 12 = 110$

Then, 90 seats are sold.

So, unsold seats = $110 - 90 = 20$
