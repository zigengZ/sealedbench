The program iterates through the list `nums` and appends each number to `kept` if it is greater than or equal to 4.

Given `nums = [6, 10, 5, 9, 12, 1]`:
- 6 >= 4 → append 6
- 10 >= 4 → append 10
- 5 >= 4 → append 5
- 9 >= 4 → append 9
- 12 >= 4 → append 12
- 1 < 4 → skip

So, `kept = [6, 10, 5, 9, 12]`

Then:
- `print(len(kept))` outputs `5`
- `print(kept)` outputs `[6, 10, 5, 9, 12]`
