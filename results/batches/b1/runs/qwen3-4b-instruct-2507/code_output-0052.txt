We have a list:  
`words = ['lilac', 'river', 'kelp', 'juniper', 'timber']`

We loop over `index` from 0 to 4 (since `len(words) = 5`):

---

**index = 1**  
- `piece = words[1] = 'river'`

---

**index = 2**

---

---

**index = 4**
