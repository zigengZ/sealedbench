We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Amber, Ash, Bay, Oak, Poe, Vale  
**Edges and weights**:
- Amber – Ash: 5  
- Amber – Bay: 7  
- Amber – Vale: 2  
- Ash – Poe: 9  
- Bay – Oak: 8  
- Bay – Poe: 3  
- Oak – Vale: 4  
- Poe – Vale: 5  

We are to find the **shortest path from Amber to Poe** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: List all possible paths from Amber to Poe

We'll consider paths through different intermediate nodes.

---

**Path 1: Amber → Ash → Poe**  
Weight: 5 (Amber–Ash) + 9 (Ash–Poe) = **14**

---

**Path 2: Amber → Bay → Poe**  
Weight: 7 (Amber–Bay) + 3 (Bay–Poe) = **10**

---

**Path 3: Amber → Vale → Poe**  
Weight: 2 (Amber–Vale) + 5 (Vale–Poe) = **7**

---

**Path 4: Amber → Bay → Oak → Vale → Poe**  
Weight: 7 + 8 + 4 + 5 = 24 → too long

**Path 5: Amber → Vale → Oak → Bay → Poe**  
Weight: 2 + 4 + 8
