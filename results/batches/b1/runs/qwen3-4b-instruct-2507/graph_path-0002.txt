We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Bay, Cleo, Hart, Nell, Quill, Sage, York  
**Edges and weights**:
- Bay – Sage: 9  
- Cleo – Hart: 4  
- Cleo – Nell: 4  
- Hart – York: 8  
- Nell – Sage: 6  
- Nell – York: 4  
- Quill – Sage: 4  
- Quill – York: 1  

We are to find the **shortest path (in terms of total weight) from Hart to Nell**.

---

### Step 1: List all possible paths from Hart to Nell

We'll consider paths through intermediate nodes.

---

- Hart to Cleo: 4  
- Cleo to Nell: 4  
- Total: 4 + 4 = **8**

---

#### Path 2: Hart → York → Nell

- York to Nell: 4  
- Total: 8 + 4 = **12**

---
