We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Bay, Cedar, Cleo, Fern, Indigo, Oak  
**Edges and weights**:

- Bay – Cleo: 9  
- Bay – Fern: 7  
- Bay – Indigo: 6  
- Bay – Oak: 1  
- Cedar – Cleo: 8  
- Cedar – Fern: 9  
- Cleo – Fern: 1  
- Cleo – Indigo: 1  
- Fern – Oak: 7  

We are to find the **shortest path (in total weight) from Fern to Indigo**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the graph structure

We can build adjacency list for clarity:

- Bay: Cleo(9), Fern(7), Indigo(6), Oak(1)  
- Cedar: Cleo(8), Fern(9)  
- Cleo: Bay(9), Cedar(8), Fern(1), Indigo(1), Oak(9)  
- Fern: Bay(7), Cedar(9), Cleo(1), Oak(7)  
- Indigo: Bay(6), Cleo(1)  
- Oak: Bay(1), Fern(7), Cleo(9)

We want the shortest path from **Fern to Indigo**.

---
