We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:

**Cedar, Lark, Moss, Oak, Poe, Xen, York, Zed**

Edges and weights:

- Cedar – Lark: 3  
- Cedar – Moss: 7  
- Cedar – Poe: 5  
- Cedar – York: 2  
- Lark – Xen: 5  
- Moss – Xen: 3  
- Moss – Zed: 8  
- Oak – Xen: 4  
- Oak – York: 4  
- Poe – York: 3  
- Xen – Zed: 2  

We are to find the **shortest path from Cedar to Oak** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

We need to find the shortest path from **Cedar** to **Oak**.

We can use **Dijkstra’s algorithm** (since all weights are positive), but since the graph is small, we can do a manual shortest path search.

Start from Cedar and explore paths to Oak.

---

### Step 2: Explore paths from Cedar to Oak

#### Path 1: Cedar → York → Oak  
- Cedar to York: 2  
- York to Oak: 4  
- Total: 2 + 4 = **6**

#### Path 2: Cedar → Poe → York → Oak  
- Cedar → Poe: 5  
- Poe → York: 3  
- York → Oak: 4  
- Total: 5 + 3 + 4 = **12**
