### Step 1: Understand the graph structure

We can build the graph and look for paths from **Ash** to **Cleo**.

From **Xen**, we can go to:
- Ash (1)
- Cedar (4)
- Cleo (4)
- Moss (5)

So from Ash → Xen → Cleo: weight = 1 + 4 = **5**
