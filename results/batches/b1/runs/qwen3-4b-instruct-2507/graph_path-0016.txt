We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:  
**Blair, Dove, Glen, Sage, Vale, Xen**

Edges and their weights:
- Blair — Dove: 5  
- Dove — Glen: 6  
- Dove — Sage: 3  
- Dove — Vale: 4  
- Dove — Xen: 7  
- Glen — Sage: 8  
- Glen — Vale: 8  
- Glen — Xen: 6  
- Sage — Vale: 2  
- Sage — Xen: 1  

We are to find the **shortest path from Blair to Glen** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

We start at **Blair**, and want to reach **Glen**.

From Blair, the only direct edge is to **Dove** (weight 5).  
So all paths from Blair must go through Dove (since no other edges from Blair are given).

So we can think of the problem as:  
**Blair → Dove → ? → Glen**

We now need to find the shortest path from Dove to Glen, and add the 5 from Blair → Dove.

So we compute the shortest path from **Dove to Glen**, then add 5.
