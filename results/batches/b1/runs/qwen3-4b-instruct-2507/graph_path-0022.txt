We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Fern, Lark, Nell, Reed, Vale, York  
**Edges and weights**:
- Fern – Reed: 6  
- Fern – Vale: 6  
- Lark – Reed: 8  
- Lark – Vale: 4  
- Nell – Vale: 3  
- Nell – York: 6  
- Reed – York: 4  

We are to find the **shortest path weight from Reed to Vale**.

---

### Step 1: List all possible paths from Reed to Vale

We'll consider direct and indirect paths.

#### Direct path:
- Reed → Vale  
  Weight = 6

#### Indirect paths:

1. Reed → Lark → Vale  
   Weight = 8 (Reed–Lark) + 4 (Lark–Vale) = 12 → longer than 6

2. Reed → York → Vale?  
   But there is **no edge from York to Vale**.  
   So this path is invalid.

3. Reed → York → Nell → Vale  
   - Reed → York: 4  
   - York → Nell: not directly given  
     We have Nell–York: 6 → so yes, York–Nell: 6  
   - Nell → Vale: 3  
   So path: Reed → York → Nell → Vale  
   Weight = 4 + 6 + 3 = 13 →
