We are given an undirected weighted graph with 10 nodes and several edges. We are to find the **shortest path weight** between **Quill** and **Sage**.

---

### Step 1: List all edges with weights

From the input:

- Ash — Nell: 5  
- Cleo — Fern: 4  
- Drew — Fern: 8  
- Fern — Jules: 7  
- Fern — Nell: 2  
- Fern — Sage: 2  
- Jules — Vale: 8  
- Nell — Quill: 8  
- Nell — Sage: 7  
- Sage — Vale: 4  
- Sage — Zed: 3  

Note: All edges are undirected.

---

### Step 2: Goal — Shortest path from **Quill** to **Sage**

We need to find the minimum total weight path from Quill to Sage.

Let’s consider possible paths from Quill to Sage.

---

### Path 1: Quill → Nell → Sage  
- Quill to Nell: 8  
- Nell to Sage: 7  
→ Total: 8 + 7 = **15**

---

### Path 2: Quill → Nell → Fern → Sage  
- Quill → Nell: 8  
- Nell → Fern: 2 (via edge Fern–Nell)  
- Fern → Sage: 2  
→ Total: 8 + 2 + 2 = **12**
