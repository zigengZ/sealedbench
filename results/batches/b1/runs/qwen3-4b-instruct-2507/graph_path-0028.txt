We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:

**Nodes**: Blair, Cleo, Eden, Fern, Kai, Poe, Tess, Xen  
**Edges and weights**:

- Blair–Cleo: 7  
- Blair–Fern: 5  
- Blair–Kai: 5  
- Blair–Tess: 9  
- Cleo–Poe: 1  
- Eden–Poe: 9  
- Eden–Tess: 6  
- Fern–Tess: 6  
- Tess–Xen: 3  

We are to find the **shortest path from Cleo to Xen**, and return the **total weight**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the connections

We need to go from **Cleo** to **Xen**.

Let’s list all possible paths from Cleo to Xen, and compute their total weights.

---

### Path 1: Cleo → Poe → Eden → Tess → Xen  
- Cleo–Poe: 1  
- Poe–Eden: 9  
- Eden–Tess: 6  
- Tess–Xen: 3  
→ Total = 1 + 9 + 6 + 3 = **19**

---

### Path 2: Cleo → Poe → Eden → Tess → Xen (same as above)

No alternative from Poe to Eden.

---

### Path 3: Cleo → Poe → ? → Tess → Xen  
Poe only connects to Cleo and Eden. So no other path from Poe.
