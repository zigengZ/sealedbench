We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Bay, Dove, Eden, Hart, Moss, Poe, Tess  
**Edges and weights**:
- Bay — Eden: 7  
- Bay — Hart: 6  
- Bay — Tess: 4  
- Dove — Hart: 3  
- Dove — Poe: 7  
- Eden — Poe: 6  
- Hart — Moss: 1  
- Poe — Tess: 7  

We are to find the **shortest path from Hart to Tess** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: Understand the graph structure

We want the shortest path from **Hart** to **Tess**.

Let’s list all possible paths from Hart to Tess and compute their weights.

---

### Path 1: Hart → Tess (direct?)
Check if there's a direct edge:  
No direct edge between Hart and Tess.  
So we must go through intermediate nodes.

---

### Path 2: Hart → Dove → Poe → Tess  
- Hart → Dove: 3  
- Dove → Poe: 7  
- Poe → Tess: 7  
Total = 3 + 7 + 7 = **17**

---

### Path 3: Hart → Bay → Tess  
- Hart → Bay: 6 (since undirected, Bay–Hart = 6)  
- Bay → Tess: 4  
Total = 6 + 4 = **10**

This is better than 17.
