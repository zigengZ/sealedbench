**Nodes**: Bay, Cedar, Cleo, Drew, Hart, Reed, Uri  
**Edges and weights**:

- Bay – Cleo: 6  
- Bay – Reed: 5  
- Bay – Uri: 8  
- Cedar – Drew: 5  
- Cleo – Hart: 1  
- Cleo – Reed: 8  
- Cleo – Uri: 8  
- Drew – Reed: 5

We are to find the **shortest path from Cleo to Drew**, and report the **total weight**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the connections

We want the shortest path from **Cleo** to **Drew**.

Let’s list all possible paths from Cleo to Drew, and compute their total weights.

---

### Path 1: Cleo → Hart → Reed → Drew  
- Cleo → Hart: 1  
- Hart → Reed: 7  
- Reed → Drew: 5  
→ Total = 1 + 7 + 5 = **13**

---

### Path 2: Cleo → Reed → Drew  
- Cleo → Reed: 8  
- Reed → Drew: 5  
→ Total = 8 + 5 = **13**
