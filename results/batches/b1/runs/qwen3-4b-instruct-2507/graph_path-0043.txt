We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:

**Cedar, Drew, Glen, Hart, Kai, Nell, Oak, Vale, West**

Edges and weights:

- Cedar–Hart: 4  
- Cedar–Kai: 9  
- Drew–Hart: 5  
- Glen–Kai: 6  
- Hart–Nell: 7  
- Hart–Vale: 6  
- Hart–West: 1  
- Kai–Oak: 8  
- Kai–West: 2  
- Oak–Vale: 9  
- Oak–West: 6  

We are to find the **shortest path from Kai to Nell**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the connections

We want the shortest path from **Kai** to **Nell**.

From the edges:

- Kai is directly connected to:
  - Cedar (9)
  - Glen (6)
  - Oak (8)
  - West (2)

- Nell is only connected to:
  - Hart (7)

So, to get from Kai to Nell, we must go through **Hart**, since Nell has only one neighbor.

So, any path from Kai to Nell must go through Hart.

Thus, we are looking for the shortest path:

**Kai → X → Hart → Nell**

Where X is any node connected to Hart or reachable from Kai via some path to Hart.
