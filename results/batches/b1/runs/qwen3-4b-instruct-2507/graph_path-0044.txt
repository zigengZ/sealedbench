We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Ash, Eden, Glen, Indigo, Moss, Oak, Tess, Vale  
**Edges**:
- Ash — Glen: 2  
- Eden — Oak: 3  
- Eden — Vale: 1  
- Glen — Indigo: 3  
- Glen — Moss: 2  
- Glen — Oak: 4  
- Glen — Vale: 6  
- Indigo — Tess: 4  
- Moss — Vale: 1  
- Oak — Vale: 8  

We are to find the **shortest path from Glen to Vale**.

---

### Step 1: List all possible paths from Glen to Vale

We start at **Glen** and want to reach **Vale**.

#### Direct edge:
- Glen → Vale: **6**

#### Through Moss:
- Glen → Moss (2), Moss → Vale (1)  
  Total: 2 + 1 = **3**

#### Through Indigo:
- Glen → Indigo (3), Indigo → Tess (4) → but Tess has no connection to Vale.  
  So this path ends at Tess, not Vale.  
  → Invalid.

#### Through Oak:
- Glen → Oak (4), Oak → Vale (8)  
  Total: 4 + 8 = **12**

#### Through Eden?
- Glen → ? Eden? No direct edge from Glen to Eden.  
- But Glen → Ash? Yes, Ash — Glen: 2
