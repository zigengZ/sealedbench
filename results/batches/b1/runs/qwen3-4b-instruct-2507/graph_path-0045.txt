We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:

**Nodes**: Ash, Bay, Cleo, Dove, Glen, Kai, Reed, Tess  
**Edges and weights**:

- Ash–Bay: 3  
- Ash–Kai: 1  
- Bay–Kai: 8  
- Bay–Tess: 5  
- Cleo–Kai: 2  
- Dove–Glen: 8  
- Dove–Tess: 9  
- Glen–Kai: 5  
- Kai–Reed: 8  
- Kai–Tess: 1  

We are to find the **shortest path from Cleo to Glen**, and report the **total weight**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

We need to go from **Cleo** to **Glen**.

Cleo is only directly connected to **Kai** (weight 2).  
Glen is directly connected to **Dove** (weight 8) and **Kai** (weight 5).

So, possible paths from Cleo to Glen:

1. **Cleo → Kai → Glen**  
   Weight: Cleo–Kai (2) + Kai–Glen (5) = **7**

2. **Cleo → Kai → Tess → Dove → Glen**  
   Cleo–Kai (2) + Kai–Tess (1) + Tess–Dove (9) + Dove–Glen (8)  
   = 2 + 1 + 9
