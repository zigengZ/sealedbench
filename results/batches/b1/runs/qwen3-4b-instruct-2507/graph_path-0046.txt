We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Ash, Blair, Dove, Drew, Oak, Reed

**Edges and weights**:
- Ash–Drew: 2  
- Ash–Reed: 3  
- Blair–Dove: 4  
- Blair–Drew: 5  
- Blair–Oak: 1  
- Blair–Reed: 1  
- Dove–Drew: 3  
- Dove–Reed: 2  
- Drew–Reed: 2  
- Oak–Reed: 8  

We are to find the **shortest path from Oak to Reed**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the connections

We want the shortest path from **Oak** to **Reed**.

Let’s list all paths from Oak to Reed, and compute their total weights.

---

### Path 1: Oak → Blair → Reed  
- Oak–Blair: 1  
- Blair–Reed: 1  
→ Total: 1 + 1 = **2**

---

### Path 2: Oak → Blair → Dove → Reed  
- Oak–Blair: 1  
- Blair–Dove: 4  
- Dove–Reed: 2  
→ Total: 1 + 4 + 2 = **7**

---

### Path 3: Oak → Blair → Drew → Reed  
- Oak–Blair: 1  
- Blair–Drew: 5  
- Drew–Reed: 2
