We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:

**Nodes**: Eden, Indigo, Lark, Oak, Quill, West, Xen, Zed  
**Edges and weights**:

- Eden–Oak: 8  
- Eden–West: 8  
- Eden–Zed: 3  
- Indigo–Oak: 2  
- Indigo–Quill: 6  
- Indigo–Zed: 4  
- Lark–Xen: 3  
- Lark–Zed: 6  
- Oak–Xen: 5  
- Oak–Zed: 4  
- Xen–Zed: 6  

We are to find the **shortest path from Quill to West**, in terms of total edge weight.

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

We need to go from **Quill** to **West**.

Quill is only directly connected to **Indigo** (weight 6).  
West is directly connected to **Eden** (weight 8).  
So, any path from Quill to West must go through Indigo → ... → Eden → West.

So possible path:  
**Quill → Indigo → ... → Eden → West**

We need to find the shortest path from Indigo to Eden, then add the 6 (Quill→Indigo) and 8 (Eden→West).

So total path weight = 6 (Quill→Indigo) + (shortest path from Indigo to Eden) + 8
