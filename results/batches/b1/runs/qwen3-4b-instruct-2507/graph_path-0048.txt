We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:  
**Eden, Hart, Lark, Quill, Reed, Xen**

Edges and their weights:
- Eden – Hart: 6  
- Eden – Xen: 1  
- Hart – Lark: 2  
- Hart – Quill: 7  
- Hart – Xen: 3  
- Lark – Quill: 4  
- Lark – Reed: 8  
- Quill – Reed: 1  
- Reed – Xen: 8  

We are to find the **shortest path weight from Eden to Hart**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the question
We are not being asked for the shortest path from Eden to any other node, but specifically **from Eden to Hart**.

So we want the shortest path between **Eden and Hart**.

Note: The graph is undirected, so all edges can be traversed in both directions.

---

### Step 2: Direct edge
There is a direct edge from **Eden to Hart** with weight **6**.

So one possible path is just:  
**Eden → Hart** → total weight = 6

Can we find a shorter path via other nodes?

Let’s consider indirect paths from Eden to Hart.

---

### Step 3: Try indirect paths

#### Path 1: Eden → Xen → Hart  
- Eden – Xen: 1  
- Xen – Hart: 3  
- Total: 1 + 3 = **4**

This is **shorter than 6
