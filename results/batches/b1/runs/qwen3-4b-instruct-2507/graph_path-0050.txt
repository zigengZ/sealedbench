We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Ash, Bay, Cedar, Glen, West, Zed  
**Edges and weights**:
- Ash – Bay: 1  
- Ash – Cedar: 2  
- Ash – Glen: 6  
- Bay – West: 7  
- Bay – Zed: 7  
- Glen – West: 5  
- Glen – Zed: 7  

We are to find the **shortest path from Ash to West** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: List all possible paths from Ash to West

We'll consider paths through different intermediate nodes.

---

**Path 1: Ash → Bay → West**  
Weight: 1 (Ash→Bay) + 7 (Bay→West) = **8**

---

**Path 2: Ash → Glen → West**  
Weight: 6 (Ash→Glen) + 5 (Glen→West) = **11**

---

**Path 3: Ash → Bay → Zed → West?**  
But Zed to West is not directly connected. Only edges are:
- Bay–Zed, Glen–Zed, and Bay–West, Glen–West.  
No direct Zed–West edge. So this path is invalid.

**Path 4: Ash → Cedar → ???**  
Cedar has no outgoing edges to any other node (only connected to Ash). So Cedar is a dead end. Can't reach West from Cedar.
