First, find the total number of marbles Farid buys:

- 9 packs with 4 marbles each:  
  $ 9 \times 4 = 36 $ marbles

- 4 packs with 3 marbles each:  
  $ 4 \times 3 = 12 $ marbles

Total marbles:  
$ 36 + 12 = 48 $ marbles

These are shared equally among 3 people:  
$ 48 \div 3 = 16 $

Each person gets 16 marbles.
