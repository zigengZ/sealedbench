Rosa starts with 7 packs * 7 ribbons/pack = 49 ribbons.
She gives 31 ribbons to Samir, so she has 49 - 31 = 18 ribbons left.
Samir gives Rosa 22 more ribbons, making her total 18 + 22 = 40 ribbons.
Each ribbon is worth 9 dollars, so 40 ribbons are worth 40 * 9 = 360 dollars.
