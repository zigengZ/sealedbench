First, let's calculate the total number of pages Hugo reads from both books.

For the first book:
- Hugo reads 9 pages a day for 5 days.
- Pages from the first book = 9 pages/day * 5 days = 45 pages.

For the second book:
- Hugo reads 15 pages a day for 3 days.
- Pages from the second book = 15 pages/day * 3 days = 45 pages.

Now, let's add the pages from both books:
Total pages = Pages from the first book + Pages from the second book
Total pages = 45 pages + 45 pages = 90 pages.

Next, Hugo skips 63 pages that were blank.

Finally, we subtract the skipped pages from the total pages:
Pages read = Total pages - Skipped pages
Pages read = 90 pages - 63 pages = 27 pages.
