First, let's calculate the total number of pages Ivy reads in one week.

**Week 1:**
- Ivy reads 19 pages a day for 5 days.
  \[
  19 \text{ pages/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 95 \text{ pages}
  \]

- Ivy reads 17 pages a day for 5 days.
  \[
  17 \text{ pages/day} \times 5 \text{ days} = 85 \text{ pages}
  \]

- Total pages read in one week:
  \[
  95 \text{ pages} + 85 \text{ pages} = 180 \text{ pages}
  \]

**Week 2:**
- Ivy skips 19 pages that were blank.
  \[
  180 \text{ pages} - 19 \text{ pages} = 161 \text{ pages}
  \]

**Total pages read in 2 weeks:**
\[
180 \text{ pages} + 161 \text{ pages} = 341 \text{ pages}
\]
