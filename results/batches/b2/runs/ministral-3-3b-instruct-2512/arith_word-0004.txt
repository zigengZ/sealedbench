Farid first buys 9 packs of marbles, each containing 4 marbles:
9 packs × 4 marbles = 36 marbles.

Next, he buys 4 packs with 3 marbles each:
4 packs × 3 marbles = 12 marbles.

Adding these together gives the total marbles:
36 + 12 = 48 marbles.

Sharing equally among 3 people:
48 ÷ 3 = 16 marbles per person.
