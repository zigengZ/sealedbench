Victor starts with pencils from the first purchase: 3 packs × 6 pencils = 18 pencils.
Then he adds pencils from the second purchase: 3 packs × 7 pencils = 21 pencils.
Total pencils before giving any away: 18 + 21 = 39 pencils.
After giving 27 pencils to Willa, Victor has 39 - 27 = 12 pencils left.
Each pencil is worth 2 dollars, so 12 pencils × 2 dollars = 24 dollars.
