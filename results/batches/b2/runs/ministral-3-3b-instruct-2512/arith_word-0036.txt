Olga starts with 4 packs of 3 crayons each: 4 × 3 = 12 crayons.
Then she buys 3 packs of 8 crayons each: 3 × 8 = 24 crayons.
Total crayons before giving any away: 12 + 24 = 36 crayons.
After giving 16 crayons to Yara: 36 - 16 = 20 crayons.
Sharing equally among 5 people: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 crayons per person.
