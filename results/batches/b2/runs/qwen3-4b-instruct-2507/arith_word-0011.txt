Chloe sets out 7 rows with 9 seats in each row.

First, find the total number of seats:

$$
7 \times 9 = 63 \text{ seats}
$$

Then, 49 seats are sold, so the number of unsold seats is:

$$
63 - 49 = 14 \text{ unsold seats}
$$

Each unsold seat is worth $7, so the total value of unsold seats is:

$$
14 \times 7 = 98 \text{ dollars}
$$
