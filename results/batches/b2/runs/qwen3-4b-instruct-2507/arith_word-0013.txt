First, find the total number of pencils Victor buys:

- 3 packs of 6 pencils:  
  $3 \times 6 = 18$ pencils

- 3 packs of 7 pencils:  
  $3 \times 7 = 21$ pencils

Total pencils before giving any away:  
$18 + 21 = 39$ pencils

Then Victor gives 27 pencils to Willa, so he keeps:  
$39 - 27 = 12$ pencils

Each pencil is worth $2, so the value of Victor's remaining pencils is:  
$12 \times 2 = 24$ dollars
