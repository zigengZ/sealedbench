Gina buys 6 packs of ribbons, and each pack has 8 ribbons.  
So, total ribbons Gina has initially:  
6 × 8 = 48 ribbons

She gives 44 ribbons to Ava, so the number of ribbons she has left is:  
48 − 44 = 4 ribbons

Each ribbon is worth $6, so the value of Gina's remaining ribbons is:  
4 × 6 = 24 dollars
