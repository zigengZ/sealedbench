First, find the total number of crayons Uma buys:

- 6 packs with 10 crayons each:  
  $6 \times 10 = 60$ crayons

- 4 packs with 7 crayons each:  
  $4 \times 7 = 28$ crayons

Add them together:  
$60 + 28 = 88$ crayons

Then, Uma gives 39 crayons to Zane:  
$88 - 39 = 49$ crayons

So, Uma has **49** crayons left.
