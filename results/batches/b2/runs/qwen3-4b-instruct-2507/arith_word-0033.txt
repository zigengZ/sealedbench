First, find the total number of erasers Ava buys:

- 7 packs with 7 erasers each:  
  $7 \times 7 = 49$ erasers

- 3 packs with 9 erasers each:  
  $3 \times 9 = 27$ erasers

Total erasers:  
$49 + 27 = 76$ erasers

These are shared equally among 2 people:  
$76 \div 2 = 38$

So, each person gets 38 erasers.
