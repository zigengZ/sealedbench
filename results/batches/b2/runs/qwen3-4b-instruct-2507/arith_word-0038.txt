Mina sets out 8 rows with 4 seats in each row.

First, find the total number of seats:

$$
8 \text{ rows} \times 4 \text{ seats per row} = 32 \text{ seats}
$$

Then, 20 seats are sold, so the number of unsold seats is:

$$
32 - 20 = 12 \text{ unsold seats}
$$

Each unsold seat is worth $8, so the total value of unsold seats is:

$$
12 \times 8 = 96 \text{ dollars}
$$
