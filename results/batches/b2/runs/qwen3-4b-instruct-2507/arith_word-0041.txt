Let's break down what Rosa reads each week and then multiply by 2 weeks.

**First book:**
- 16 pages/day for 2 days  
= 16 × 2 = 32 pages

**Second book:**
- 16 pages/day for 5 days  
= 16 × 5 = 80 pages

**Total pages read in one week (before skipping):**  
32 + 80 = 112 pages

**Then, Rosa skips 13 blank pages** — these don't count, so we subtract them:  
112 − 13 = 99 pages per week

She repeats this **for 2 weeks**:  
99 × 2 = 198 pages
