First, find the total number of stickers Yara buys:

- 3 packs of 12 stickers:  
  $3 \times 12 = 36$ stickers

- 4 packs of 2 stickers:  
  $4 \times 2 = 8$ stickers

Total stickers:  
$36 + 8 = 44$ stickers

Yara gives 36 stickers to Ben, so she has:  
$44 - 36 = 8$ stickers left

Each sticker is worth $4, so the value of her remaining stickers is:  
$8 \times 4 = 32$ dollars
