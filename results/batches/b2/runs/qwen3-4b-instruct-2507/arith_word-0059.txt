Jamal buys 5 packs of stamps, and each pack has 6 stamps:

5 × 6 = 30 stamps

Then he gives 14 stamps to Felix:

30 − 14 = 16 stamps left with Jamal

Then Felix gives Jamal 20 more stamps:

16 + 20 = 36 stamps now with Jamal

Each stamp is worth $2, so:

36 × 2 = 72 dollars
