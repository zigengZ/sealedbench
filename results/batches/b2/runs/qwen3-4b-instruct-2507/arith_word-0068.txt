1. **First book**: 19 pages/day for 5 days  
   $ 19 \times 5 = 95 $ pages

2. **Second book**: 17 pages/day for 5 days  
   $ 17 \times 5 = 85 $ pages

3. **Total pages read in one week (before skipping)**:  
   $ 95 + 85 = 180 $ pages

4. **Ivy skips 19 blank pages** — these don't count, so we subtract them:  
   $ 180 - 19 = 161 $ pages per week

5. **She repeats this for 2 weeks**:  
   $ 161 \times 2 = 322 $ pages
