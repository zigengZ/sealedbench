Let's break down how many pages Victor reads in one week:

1. **First book**:  
   17 pages/day × 4 days = 68 pages

2. **Second book**:  
   18 pages/day × 2 days = 36 pages

3. **Total pages read in one week (before skipping)**:  
   68 + 36 = 104 pages

4. **Victor skips 34 blank pages** — these don't count, so we subtract them:  
   104 - 34 = 70 pages per week

5. **He repeats this for 4 weeks**:  
   70 pages/week × 4 weeks = 280 pages
