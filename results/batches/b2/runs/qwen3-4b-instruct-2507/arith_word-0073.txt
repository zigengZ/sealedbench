Leo buys 4 packs of tokens, and each pack has 4 tokens.  
So, total tokens Leo has:  
4 packs × 4 tokens = 16 tokens  

Then Leo gives 8 tokens to Hugo, so he keeps:  
16 − 8 = 8 tokens  

Each token is worth $9, so the value of Leo's remaining tokens is:  
8 tokens × $9 = $72
