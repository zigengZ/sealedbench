First, find the total number of crayons Tara buys:

- 4 packs of 8 crayons:  
  $4 \times 8 = 32$ crayons

- 4 packs of 3 crayons:  
  $4 \times 3 = 12$ crayons

Total crayons before giving any away:  
$32 + 12 = 44$ crayons

Tara gives 3 crayons to Nico, so she has:  
$44 - 3 = 41$ crayons left

Each crayon is worth $7, so the total value of Tara's crayons is:  
$41 \times 7 = 287$ dollars
