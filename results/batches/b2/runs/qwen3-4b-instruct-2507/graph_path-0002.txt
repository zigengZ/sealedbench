We are given an undirected weighted graph with 10 nodes and several edges. We are to find the **shortest path from Amber to Reed** in terms of total edge weight.

---

### Step 1: List all edges with weights

From the input:

- Amber – Glen: 4  
- Amber – Poe: 1  
- Amber – Tess: 2  
- Dove – Reed: 6  
- Dove – York: 6  
- Glen – Quill: 4  
- Glen – York: 3  
- Nell – Oak: 8  
- Nell – Quill: 5  
- Nell – York: 3  
- Oak – Reed: 1  

Note: All edges are undirected.

---

### Step 2: Goal — Shortest path from **Amber** to **Reed**

We will explore possible paths from Amber to Reed, using the available edges.

---

### Step 3: Explore paths from Amber to Reed

#### Path 1: Amber → Poe  
- Amber to Poe: 1  
- Poe to Reed? → No direct edge.  
- Poe to others? Not connected to Reed directly.  
→ Need to go from Poe to somewhere else?

Check connections of Poe: only Amber. So Poe is a leaf node.  
→ Cannot reach Reed from Poe.

#### Path 2: Amber → Tess  
- Amber to Tess: 2  
- Tess has no other connections → dead end.

#### Path 3: Amber → Glen  
- Amber to Glen: 4  
- From Glen:  
  - Glen → Quill: 4  
  - Glen → York: 3  

So from Glen, we can go to Quill or York.

##### Option A: Amber → Glen → York

- From York → Reed?

  - York – Dove: 6

  - York – Oak? Not listed  
  - York – Quill? Not listed  
  - So from York: can go to Dove or Nell  

From York → Dove: 6  
Dove → Reed: 6  
So: Amber → Glen → York → Dove → Reed  
Total: 4 + 3 + 6 + 6 = **19**

From York → Nell: 3  
Nell → Oak: 8  
Oak → Reed: 1  
So: Amber → Glen → York → Nell → Oak → Reed  
Total: 4 + 3 + 3 + 8 + 1 = **19**

Same total.
