We are given an undirected weighted graph with 9 nodes and several edges. We are to find the **shortest path from Ash to York** in terms of total edge weight.

---

### Step 1: List all nodes
- Ash, Cleo, Eden, Hart, Lark, Moss, Reed, Uri, York

---

### Step 2: List all edges with weights

- Ash — Eden: 1  
- Ash — Hart: 8  
- Cleo — Uri: 6  
- Eden — Lark: 7  
- Eden — Moss: 5  
- Hart — Moss: 5  
- Hart — Reed: 9  
- Hart — Uri: 6  
- Hart — York: 6  
- Lark — Reed: 2  
- Reed — Uri: 4  
- Uri — York: 1  

---

### Step 3: Goal — Shortest path from **Ash to York**

We will use **Dijkstra’s algorithm** (since all weights are positive) to compute the shortest path from Ash to York.

We start at Ash and compute shortest distances to all other nodes.

---

### Initialize distances:

- dist[Ash] = 0  
- All others: ∞

---

### Step-by-step Dijkstra:

**Step 1: From Ash**

From Ash, we can go to:
- Eden (1)
- Hart (8)

Update:
- dist[Eden] = 1
- dist[Hart] = 8

Queue: Eden (1), Hart (8)

---

**Step 2: Process Eden (distance 1)**

From Eden:
- Eden → Lark: 7 → Lark = 1 + 7 = 8
- Eden → Moss: 5 → Moss = 1 + 5 = 6
- Eden → (no other)

Update:
- dist[Lark] = 8
- dist[Moss] = 6

Queue now: Hart (8), Moss (6), Lark (8)

---

**Step 3: Process Moss (distance 6)**

From Moss:
- Moss → Hart: 5 → Hart = min(8, 6+5=11) → still 8
- Moss → (no other)

No improvement.

Queue: Hart (8), Lark (8)

---

**Step 4: Process Hart (distance 8)**

From Hart:
- Hart → Reed: 9 → Reed = 8 + 9 = 17
- Hart → Uri: 6 → Uri = 8 + 6 = 14
- Hart → York: 6 → York = 8 + 6 = 14

Update:
- dist[Reed] = 17
- dist[Uri] = 14
- dist[York] = 14

Queue: Lark (8), Reed (17), Uri (14)

---

**Step 5: Process Lark (distance 8)**

From Lark:
- Lark → Reed: 2 → Reed = min(17, 8 + 2 = 10) → update Reed to 10

Update:
- dist[Reed] = 10

Queue: Uri (14), Reed (10)

---

- Uri → York: 1 → York = min(14, 14 + 1 = 15) → still 14
