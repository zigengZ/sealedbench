We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Dove, Eden, Jules, Poe, Tess, Vale  
**Edges and weights**:
- Dove – Jules: 4  
- Dove – Poe: 8  
- Eden – Jules: 8  
- Eden – Poe: 6  
- Jules – Poe: 1  
- Jules – Tess: 9  
- Poe – Tess: 4  
- Tess – Vale: 4  

We are to find the **shortest path from Jules to Tess**.

---

### Step 1: List all possible paths from Jules to Tess

We can go directly:
- **Jules → Tess**: weight = 9

Other paths:
1. **Jules → Poe → Tess**  
   - Jules to Poe: 1  
   - Poe to Tess: 4  
   - Total: 1 + 4 = **5**

2. **Jules → Eden → Poe → Tess**  
   - Jules → Eden: 8  
   - Eden → Poe: 6  
   - Poe → Tess: 4  
   - Total: 8 + 6 + 4 = 18 → too long

3. **Jules → Dove → Poe → Tess**  
   - Jules → Dove: 4  
   - Dove → Poe: 8  
   - Poe → Tess: 4  
   - Total: 4 + 8 + 4 = 16 → too long

4. **Jules → Poe → Tess** (already considered) → 5

5. **Jules → Eden → Poe → Tess** → already considered → 18

6. Any path through Vale?  
   - Tess → Vale is 4, but we are going to Tess, not from Tess. So going through Vale adds unnecessary weight.

So the only viable paths are:
- Direct: 9  
- Jules → Poe → Tess: 1 + 4 = 5

Thus, the **shortest path** is **Jules → Poe → Tess** with total weight **5**.
