We are given an undirected weighted graph with 10 nodes and several edges. We are to find the **shortest path weight** from **Fern** to **Poe**.

---

### Step 1: List all nodes
Nodes:  
Cedar, Fern, Indigo, Jules, Nell, Poe, Quill, Reed, Tess, Zed

---

### Step 2: List all edges with weights

- Cedar — Fern: 8  
- Cedar — Jules: 1  
- Cedar — Tess: 9  
- Fern — Quill: 4  
- Indigo — Tess: 8  
- Jules — Poe: 6  
- Jules — Reed: 9  
- Jules — Tess: 6  
- Nell — Tess: 1  
- Poe — Quill: 2  
- Poe — Zed: 3  
- Reed — Tess: 9  
- Tess — Zed: 7  

---

### Step 3: Goal — Shortest path from **Fern** to **Poe**

We will use **Dijkstra’s algorithm** (since all weights are positive) to compute the shortest path from Fern to Poe.

We start at **Fern** and compute shortest distances to all other nodes.

---

### Step 4: Build adjacency list

We'll build the graph as an undirected graph.

- Fern: [Cedar (8), Quill (4)]  
- Cedar: [Fern (8), Jules (1), Tess (9)]  
- Jules: [Cedar (1), Poe (6), Tess (6), Reed (9)]  
- Poe: [Jules (6), Quill (2), Zed (3)]  
- Quill: [Fern (4), Poe (2)]  
- Tess: [Cedar (9), Jules (6), Indigo (8), Nell (1), Zed (7)]  
- Indigo: [Tess (8)]  
- Nell: [Tess (1)]  
- Reed: [Jules (9), Tess (9)]  
- Zed: [Poe (3), Tess (7)]

---

### Step 5: Run Dijkstra from Fern

Initialize distances:
- dist[Fern] = 0  
- All others = ∞

**Queue**: (Fern, 0)

---

**1. Process Fern (0)**  
From Fern:
- To Cedar: 8 → dist[Cedar] = 8  
- To Quill: 4 → dist[Quill] = 4  

Queue: (Cedar, 8), (Quill, 4)

---

**2. Process Quill (4)**  
From Quill:
- To Fern: already visited  
- To Poe: 2 → dist[Poe] = 4 + 2 = 6  

Now dist[Poe] = 6

Queue: (Cedar, 8)

---

**3. Process Cedar (8)**  
From Cedar:
- To Fern: already visited  
- To Jules: 1 → new path to Jules: 8 + 1 = 9  
- To Tess: 9 → new path to Tess: 8 + 9 = 17  

Update:
- dist[Jules] = 9  
- dist[Tess] = 17  

Queue: (Jules, 9), (Tess, 17)

---

**4. Process Jules (9)**  
From Jules:
- To Poe: 6 → current dist[Poe] = 6, new path = 9 + 6 = 1
