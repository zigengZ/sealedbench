We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Amber, Ash, Hart, Lark, Quill, York  
**Edges and weights**:
- Amber – Ash: 9  
- Amber – Hart: 5  
- Amber – Quill: 9  
- Ash – Lark: 1  
- Ash – Quill: 9  
- Ash – York: 4  
- Lark – York: 4  

We are to find the **shortest path weight from Hart to Quill**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the graph structure

We can build the connections:

- Hart is connected to Amber (5)
- Amber is connected to Ash (9), Quill (9), Hart (5)
- Ash is connected to Lark (1), Quill (9), York (4)
- Lark is connected to York (4)
- York is connected to Ash (4), Lark (4)

We want the shortest path from **Hart to Quill**.

---

### Step 2: Use BFS or Dijkstra (since all weights are positive)

We can use Dijkstra’s algorithm to compute shortest paths from Hart to all other nodes.

Start at **Hart**.

#### Initialize:
- Distance to Hart: 0
- All others: ∞

#### Step-by-step propagation:

1. From Hart:
   - Edge to Amber: weight 5 → distance to Amber = 5

2. From Amber:
   - To Ash: 9 → total = 5 + 9 = 14
   - To Quill: 9 → total = 5 + 9 = 14
   - To Hart: already visited

3. From Ash (distance 14):
   - To Lark: 1 → total = 14 + 1 = 15
   - To Quill: 9 → total = 14 + 9 = 23 → but we already have Quill at 14 via Amber → so 14 is better
   - To York: 4 → total = 14 + 4 = 18

4. From Lark (distance 15):
   - To York: 4 → total = 15 + 4 = 19 → but York already has 18 → so update only if better (19 > 18, skip)

5. From York (distance 18):
   - Already connected to Ash and Lark → no new paths

Now, we have:
- Quill: reached via Amber → 5 (Hart → Amber) + 9 (Amber → Quill) = **14**

Is there a shorter path?

Alternative path?
- Hart → Amber → Ash → Quill: 5 + 9 + 9 = 23 → worse
- Hart → Amber → Ash → Lark → York → ??? → no direct edge to Quill
- Hart → Amber → Ash → York → ??? → no edge to Quill

So only direct path from Hart to Quill is via Amber.

Any other path?

What about Hart → Amber → Ash → Lark → York → ??? → no connection to Quill.

So no alternative.

Thus, the shortest path is:
**Hart → Amber → Quill**  
Total weight: 5 + 9 = **14**

#### Final Answer:
#### 14 [end of text]
