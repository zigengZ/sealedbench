Let's break down how many pages Felix reads each week.

**First book:**  
Felix reads 20 pages a day for 4 days:  
$ 20 \times 4 = 80 $ pages

**Second book:**  
Felix reads 17 pages a day for 2 days:  
$ 17 \times 2 = 34 $ pages

**Total pages read in one week (before skipping):**  
$ 80 + 34 = 114 $ pages

**Skipping 80 blank pages:**  
These don't count, so we subtract them:  
$ 114 - 80 = 34 $ pages actually read per week

Now, Felix repeats this **each week for 3 weeks**:  
$ 34 \times 3 = 102 $ pages

#### 102 [end of text]
