First, let's calculate the total number of pages Felix reads in one week.

**Week 1:**
- Pages read from the first book: \(20 \text{ pages/day} \times 4 \text{ days} = 80 \text{ pages}\)
- Pages read from the second book: \(17 \text{ pages/day} \times 2 \text{ days} = 34 \text{ pages}\)
- Total pages read in Week 1: \(80 + 34 = 114 \text{ pages}\)

**Week 2:**
- Pages read from the first book: \(20 \text{ pages/day} \times 4 \text{ days} = 80 \text{ pages}\)
- Pages read from the second book: \(17 \text{ pages/day} \times 2 \text{ days} = 34 \text{ pages}\)
- Total pages read in Week 2: \(80 + 34 = 114 \text{ pages}\)

**Week 3:**
- Total pages read in Week 1 and Week 2: \(114 + 114 = 228 \text{ pages}\)

**Total pages read in 3 weeks:**
\[ 228 \text{ pages/week} \times 3 \text{ weeks} = 684 \text{ pages} \]
