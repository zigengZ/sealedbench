First, let's calculate the total number of ribbons Olga initially had.

She bought 3 packs of ribbons, and each pack contains 12 ribbons.

So, the total number of ribbons Olga had initially is:
3 packs * 12 ribbons/pack = 36 ribbons.

Then, Olga gives 17 ribbons to Boris.

To find out how many ribbons Olga has left, we subtract the ribbons given to Boris from the initial total:
36 ribbons - 17 ribbons = 19 ribbons.
