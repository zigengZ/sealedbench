First, let's calculate the total number of badges Felix has after the transactions.

Felix buys 5 packs of badges, and each pack contains 8 badges.
So, the total number of badges Felix has initially is:
5 packs * 8 badges/pack = 40 badges

Next, Felix gives 25 badges to Nico.
So, the number of badges Felix has left is:
40 badges - 25 badges = 15 badges

Then, Nico gives Felix 23 more badges.
So, the total number of badges Felix has now is:
15 badges + 23 badges = 38 badges

Each badge is worth 9 dollars.
To find the total value of Felix's badges, we multiply the number of badges by the value per badge:
38 badges * 9 dollars/badge = 342 dollars
