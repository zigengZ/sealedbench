First, let's calculate the total number of balloons Kira initially has.

Kira buys 7 packs of balloons, and each pack contains 4 balloons.

Total balloons = Number of packs × Balloons per pack
Total balloons = 7 packs × 4 balloons/pack
Total balloons = 28 balloons

Kira then gives 4 balloons to Hugo.

Remaining balloons = Total balloons - Balloons given to Hugo
Remaining balloons = 28 balloons - 4 balloons
Remaining balloons = 24 balloons

So, Kira has #### 24 balloons. [end of text]
