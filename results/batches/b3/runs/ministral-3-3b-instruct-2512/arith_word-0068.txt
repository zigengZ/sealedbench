Willa reads **11 pages/day × 6 days = 66 pages** from the first book.
She reads **6 pages/day × 4 days = 24 pages** from the second book.
Total pages before skipping: **66 + 24 = 90 pages**.
After skipping 28 blank pages: **90 - 28 = 62 pages per week**.
For 4 weeks: **62 × 4 = 248 pages**.
