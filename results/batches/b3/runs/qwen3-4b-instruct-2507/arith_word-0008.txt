First, find the total number of cards Mina buys:

- 9 packs × 11 cards = 99 cards  
- 6 packs × 2 cards = 12 cards  

Total cards = 99 + 12 = 111 cards

Then Mina gives 49 cards to Ben, so:

111 - 49 = 62 cards left

These 62 cards are shared equally among 2 people:

62 ÷ 2 = 31

So each person gets 31 cards.
