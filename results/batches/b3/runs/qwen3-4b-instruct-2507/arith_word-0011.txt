- 4 rows with 5 seats each:  
  $4 \times 5 = 20$ seats

- 6 rows with 7 seats each:  
  $6 \times 7 = 42$ seats

Total seats = $20 + 42 = 62$ seats

Then, 39 seats are sold, so the number of unsold seats is:  
$62 - 39 = 23$ seats

$23 \times 8 = 184$ dollars
