Let's break down how many pages Aisha reads in one week.

**First book:**  
She reads 23 pages a day for 3 days:  
$ 23 \times 3 = 69 $ pages

**Second book:**  
She reads 17 pages a day for 4 days:  
$ 17 \times 4 = 68 $ pages

**Skip pages:**  
She skips 108 blank pages — these do **not** count toward reading.

So, total pages read in **one week** (before skipping):  
$ 69 + 68 = 137 $ pages  
But she skips 108 pages — so actual pages read:  
$ 137 - 108 = 29 $ pages per week

Now, she repeats this **for 2 weeks**:  
$ 29 \times 2 = 58 $ pages
