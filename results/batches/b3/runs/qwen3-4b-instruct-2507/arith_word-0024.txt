Tara buys 6 packs of pencils, and each pack has 7 pencils:

6 × 7 = 42 pencils

Then Tara gives 18 pencils to Hugo, so she has:

42 − 18 = 24 pencils left

Then Hugo gives Tara 4 more pencils, so Tara now has:

24 + 4 = 28 pencils

Each pencil is worth $3, so the total value of Tara's pencils is:

28 × 3 = 84 dollars
