Dmitri buys 4 packs of erasers, and each pack has 8 erasers:

4 × 8 = 32 erasers

Then he gives 17 erasers to Esme:

32 − 17 = 15 erasers left with Dmitri

Then Esme gives Dmitri 18 more erasers:

15 + 18 = 33 erasers now with Dmitri

Each eraser is worth $3, so the total value is:

33 × 3 = 99 dollars
