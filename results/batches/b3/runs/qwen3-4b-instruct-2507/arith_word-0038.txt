First, find the total number of stamps Xavier buys:

- 6 packs with 3 stamps each:  
  $6 \times 3 = 18$ stamps

- 2 packs with 6 stamps each:  
  $2 \times 6 = 12$ stamps

Total stamps:  
$18 + 12 = 30$ stamps

These are shared equally among 3 people:  
$30 \div 3 = 10$

Each person gets 10 stamps.
