First, find the total number of seats Diego sets up.

He sets out:
- 7 rows with 14 seats each:  
  $7 \times 14 = 98$ seats

Then he adds:
- 8 rows with 5 seats each:  
  $8 \times 5 = 40$ seats

Total seats = $98 + 40 = 138$ seats

Then, 39 seats are sold, so the number of **unsold seats** is:  
$138 - 39 = 99$ seats

Each unsold seat is worth $7, so the total value is:  
$99 \times 7 = 693$ dollars
