First, find the total number of stamps Chloe buys:

- 8 packs with 11 stamps each:  
  $ 8 \times 11 = 88 $ stamps

- 4 packs with 2 stamps each:  
  $ 4 \times 2 = 8 $ stamps

Total stamps:  
$ 88 + 8 = 96 $ stamps

These are shared equally among 3 people:  
$ 96 \div 3 = 32 $

Each person gets 32 stamps.
