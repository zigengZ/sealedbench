Let's break down how many pages Willa reads in one week:

1. **First book**:  
   11 pages/day × 6 days = 66 pages

2. **Second book**:  
   6 pages/day × 4 days = 24 pages

3. **Total pages read in one week (before skipping)**:  
   66 + 24 = 90 pages

4. **She skips 28 blank pages** — these don't count, so we subtract them:  
   90 - 28 = 62 pages per week

5. **She repeats this for 4 weeks**:  
   62 pages/week × 4 weeks = 248 pages
