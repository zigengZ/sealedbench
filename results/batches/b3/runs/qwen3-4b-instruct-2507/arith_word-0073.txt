First, find the total number of magnets Ivy buys:

- 2 packs with 12 magnets each:  
  $2 \times 12 = 24$ magnets

- 3 packs with 7 magnets each:  
  $3 \times 7 = 21$ magnets

Total magnets:  
$24 + 21 = 45$ magnets

These are shared equally among 3 people:  
$45 \div 3 = 15$

So, each person gets 15 magnets.
