Let's break down how many pages Elena reads each week.

**First book:**  
She reads 16 pages a day for 6 days:  
16 × 6 = 96 pages

**Second book:**  
She reads 10 pages a day for 3 days:  
10 × 3 = 30 pages

**Total pages read in one week (before skipping):**  
96 + 30 = 126 pages

**She skips 31 blank pages** — these don't count, so we subtract them:  
126 − 31 = 95 pages per week (actual pages read)

Now, she repeats this **each week for 3 weeks**:  
95 × 3 = 285 pages
