We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Cedar, Drew, Glen, Nell, Poe, Quill  
**Edges and weights**:
- Cedar – Drew: 7  
- Drew – Glen: 7  
- Drew – Quill: 5  
- Glen – Poe: 2  
- Glen – Quill: 9  
- Nell – Quill: 8  
- Poe – Quill: 9  

We are to find the **shortest path from Cedar to Poe** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: Understand the connections

We want to go from **Cedar → Poe**.

Let’s list possible paths from Cedar to Poe:

---

**Path 1: Cedar → Drew → Glen → Poe**  
- Cedar–Drew: 7  
- Drew–Glen: 7  
- Glen–Poe: 2  
→ Total = 7 + 7 + 2 = **16**

---

**Path 2: Cedar → Drew → Quill → Poe**  
- Cedar–Drew: 7  
- Drew–Quill: 5  
- Quill–Poe: 9  
→ Total = 7 + 5 + 9 = **21**

---

**Path 3: Cedar → Drew → Glen → Quill → Poe**  
- Cedar–Drew: 7  
- Drew–Glen: 7  
- Glen–Quill: 9  
- Quill–Poe: 9  
→ Total = 7 + 7 + 9 + 9 = **32** → too long

---

**Path 4: Cedar → Drew → Quill → Nell → ???**  
No connection from Nell to anyone else. Nell only connects to Quill. So no path from Nell to Poe. This path is dead end.

---

**Path 5: Any path through Nell?**  
Nell only connects to Quill. So unless we go through Quill, we can't reach Poe from Nell. But that doesn't help us go from Cedar to Poe directly.

So only viable paths are the ones above.

---

### Compare all viable paths:

- Cedar → Drew → Glen → Poe: **16**
- Cedar → Drew → Quill → Poe: **21**

No other paths exist.

Thus, the **shortest path** is **16**.

#### 16 [end of text]
