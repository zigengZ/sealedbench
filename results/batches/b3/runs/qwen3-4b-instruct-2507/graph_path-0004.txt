### Step 1: Understand the graph structure

We need to find the shortest path from **Dove** to **Uri**.

Let’s list all edges again with weights:

- Dove ↔ Glen: 5

Note: All edges are undirected.

We can now consider possible paths from **Dove** to **Uri**.
