We are given an undirected weighted graph with 10 nodes and several edges. We are to find the **shortest path from Bay to Quill** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: List all edges with weights

From the input:

- Ash — Hart: 7  
- Bay — Glen: 8  
- Bay — Kai: 5  
- Dove — Quill: 4  
- Dove — Reed: 7  
- Dove — Sage: 8  
- Glen — Kai: 5  
- Glen — Lark: 3  
- Hart — Lark: 7  
- Hart — Quill: 8  
- Kai — Reed: 6  
- Quill — Sage: 6  

---

### Step 2: Goal — Shortest path from **Bay** to **Quill**

We will use **Dijkstra’s algorithm** conceptually (since all weights are positive) to find the shortest path from Bay to Quill.

We start at Bay and explore paths to Quill.

---

### Step 3: Build adjacency list (for clarity)

- Bay: Glen (8), Kai (5)  
- Glen: Bay (8), Kai (5), Lark (3)  
- Kai: Bay (5), Glen (5), Reed (6)  
- Reed: Kai (6), Dove (7)  
- Dove: Quill (4), Reed (7), Sage (8)  
- Sage: Dove (8), Quill (6)  
- Quill: Dove (4), Sage (6), Hart (8)  
- Hart: Ash (7), Lark (7), Quill (8)  
- Lark: Glen (3), Hart (7)  
- Ash: Hart (7)

---

### Step 4: Run Dijkstra from Bay to Quill

We maintain distances from Bay to each node.

Initialize:

- dist[Bay] = 0  
- All others = ∞

We process nodes in order of increasing distance.

---

**Step 1: Start at Bay (0)**

From Bay:
- To Glen: 8 → dist[Glen] = 8  
- To Kai: 5 → dist[Kai] = 5

Queue: Glen (8), Kai (5)

---

**Step 2: Process Kai (5)**

From Kai:
- To Bay: already visited  
- To Glen: 5 → current Glen is 8 → update Glen to 5 + 5 = 10? Wait — no: Kai to Glen is 5, so from Bay to Kai to Glen = 5 + 5 = 10, but Glen already has 8 (via Bay), so 8 < 10 → keep 8  
- To Reed: 6 → dist[Reed] = 5 + 6 = 11

Now dist[Reed] = 11

Queue: Glen (8), Reed (11)

---

**Step 3: Process Glen (8)**

From Glen:
- To Bay: already visited  
- To Kai: already visited  
- To Lark: 3 → dist[Lark] = 8 + 3 = 11

Now dist[Lark] = 11

Queue: Reed (11), Lark (11)

---

**Step 4: Process Reed (11)**

From Reed:
- To Kai: already visited  
- To Dove: 7 → dist[Dove] = 11 + 7 = 18

Now dist[Dove] = 18

Queue: Lark (11), Dove (18)
