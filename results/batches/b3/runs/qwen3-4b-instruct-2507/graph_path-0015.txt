We are given an undirected weighted graph with 10 nodes and several edges. We are to find the **shortest path between Reed and Xen**, and report the **total weight** of that path.

---

### Step 1: List all nodes
Nodes: Amber, Ash, Cedar, Dove, Eden, Glen, Nell, Reed, Xen, York

We are to find the shortest path from **Reed to Xen**.

---

### Step 2: List all edges with weights

From the input:

- Amber – Ash: 2  
- Amber – Nell: 2  
- Ash – Cedar: 2  
- Cedar – Eden: 5  
- Cedar – Glen: 4  
- Cedar – Nell: 4  
- Cedar – Xen: 2  
- Dove – Eden: 4  
- Dove – Reed: 6  
- Eden – Glen: 3  
- Eden – Reed: 3  
- Eden – Xen: 9  
- Eden – York: 8  

Note: All edges are undirected.

---

### Step 3: Focus on path from Reed to Xen

We want the shortest path from **Reed** to **Xen**.

Let’s consider possible paths.

---

#### Option 1: Direct edge?
Is there a direct edge between Reed and Xen?  
No. The edges involving Reed:  
- Reed – Dove: 6  
- Reed – Eden: 3  

Edges involving Xen:  
- Xen – Cedar: 2  
- Xen – Eden: 9  

So no direct Reed–Xen edge.

---

#### Option 2: Reed → Eden → Xen  
- Reed to Eden: 3  
- Eden to Xen: 9  
→ Total: 3 + 9 = **12**

---

#### Option 3: Reed → Dove → Eden → Xen  
- Reed → Dove: 6  
- Dove → Eden: 4  
- Eden → Xen: 9  
→ Total: 6 + 4 + 9 = **19** → worse

---

#### Option 4: Reed → Eden → Glen → Cedar → Xen  
- Reed → Eden: 3  
- Eden → Glen: 3  
- Glen → Cedar: 4 (since Cedar–Glen is 4)  
- Cedar → Xen: 2  
→ Total: 3 + 3 + 4 + 2 = **12**

Same as Option 2.

---

#### Option 5: Reed → Eden → Cedar → Xen  
- Reed → Eden: 3  
- Eden → Cedar: 5  
- Cedar → Xen: 2  
→ Total: 3 + 5 + 2 = **10**

Wait — is Eden–Cedar an edge? Yes: Cedar–Eden: 5 → valid.

So this path: **Reed → Eden → Cedar → Xen**  
Weight: 3 + 5 + 2 = **10**

Better than previous 12.

Is this valid? Yes.

---

#### Option 6: Reed → Dove → Eden → Cedar → Xen  
- Reed → Dove: 6  
- Dove → Eden: 4  
- Eden → Cedar: 5  
- Cedar → Xen: 2  
→ 6 + 4 + 5 + 2 = 17 → worse

---

#### Option 7: Reed → Eden → Glen → Cedar → Xen  
- Reed → Eden: 3  
- Eden → Glen: 3  
- Glen → Cedar: 4  
- Cedar → Xen: 2  
→ 3 + 3 + 4 + 2 = 12 → worse than 10
