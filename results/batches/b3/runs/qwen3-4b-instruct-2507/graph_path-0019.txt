We are to find the **shortest path from Fern to Glen**, and report the **total weight**.

### Step 1: Understand the connections

We need to go from **Fern** to **Glen**.

Fern is only directly connected to **Hart** (weight 9).

Glen is only directly connected to **Sage** (weight 9).

So, one possible path is:

**Fern → Hart → Sage → Glen**  
Weights:  
- Fern–Hart: 9  
- Hart–Sage: 7  
- Sage–Glen: 9  
→ Total: 9 + 7 + 9 = **25**

But is there a shorter path?

Let’s explore if we can go through other nodes to reduce the total.

Note: All other nodes (Bay, Moss, Poe, Xen) are not directly connected to Fern or Glen, so we must go through intermediate nodes.
