We are given an undirected weighted graph with nodes:

**Cleo, Dove, Fern, Hart, Lark, Nell, Poe, Quill**

Edges and weights:

- Cleo — Hart: 9  
- Cleo — Quill: 5  
- Dove — Lark: 9  
- Fern — Hart: 8  
- Fern — Quill: 8  
- Hart — Lark: 4  
- Hart — Poe: 9  
- Lark — Nell: 9  
- Lark — Poe: 9  
- Poe — Quill: 5  

We are to find the **shortest path from Cleo to Dove**, and report the **total weight**.

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

We need to go from **Cleo** to **Dove**.

Direct edge?  
No direct edge between Cleo and Dove.

So we must go through intermediate nodes.

We can use **Dijkstra’s algorithm** or manually explore shortest paths.

Let’s try to find possible paths from Cleo to Dove.

---

### Step 2: Explore possible paths from Cleo to Dove

#### Path 1: Cleo → Hart → Lark → Dove  
- Cleo → Hart: 9  
- Hart → Lark: 4  
- Lark → Dove: 9  
→ Total: 9 + 4 + 9 = **22**

#### Path 2: Cleo → Quill → Fern → Hart → Dove?  
But no edge from Fern to Dove.  
Fern → Hart: 8, Hart → Lark → Dove: 4 + 9 = 13 → total:  
Cleo → Quill: 5  
Quill → Fern: 8  
Fern → Hart: 8  
Hart → Lark → Dove: 4 + 9 = 13  
Total: 5 + 8 + 8 + 4 + 9 = **34** → too long

#### Path 3: Cleo → Quill → Poe → Lark → Dove
