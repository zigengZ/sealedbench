We are given an undirected weighted graph with 9 nodes:

**Nodes**: Blair, Fern, Jules, Kai, Moss, Quill, Tess, Uri, Vale  
We are to find the **shortest path weight from Blair to Quill**.

---

### Step 1: List all edges with weights

From the input:

- Blair — Tess: 8  
- Fern — Kai: 8  
- Fern — Uri: 9  
- Jules — Quill: 5  
- Jules — Tess: 2  
- Jules — Uri: 5  
- Jules — Vale: 7  
- Kai — Uri: 7  
- Kai — Vale: 4  
- Moss — Uri: 7  
- Quill — Tess: 7  
- Quill — Vale: 5  
- Tess — Uri: 2  

Note: All edges are undirected, so each edge appears once.

---

### Step 2: Goal — Shortest path from **Blair** to **Quill**

We will perform a shortest path algorithm (like Dijkstra’s) starting from Blair.

We need to compute the shortest distance from Blair to Quill.

---

### Step 3: Build adjacency list

Let’s build the adjacency list:

- Blair: [Tess: 8]  
- Fern: [Kai: 8, Uri: 9]  
- Jules: [Quill: 5, Tess: 2, Uri: 5, Vale: 7]  
- Kai: [Fern: 8, Uri: 7, Vale: 4]  
- Moss: [Uri: 7]  
- Quill: [Jules: 5, Tess: 7, Vale: 5]  
- Tess: [Blair: 8, Jules: 2, Uri: 2, Quill: 7]  
- Uri: [Fern: 9, Jules: 5, Kai: 7, Moss: 7, Tess: 2]  
- Vale: [Jules: 7, Kai: 4, Quill: 5]

---

### Step 4: Run Dijkstra’s algorithm from Blair

Initialize distances:

- dist[Blair] = 0  
- All others = ∞

Queue: (0, Blair)

---

**Step 1**: Process Blair (dist = 0)

From Blair → Tess (8)

Update:
- dist[Tess] = 8

Queue: (8, Tess)

---

**Step 2**: Process Tess (dist = 8)

From Tess:
- To Blair: already visited
- To Jules: 2 → new path: 8 + 2 = 10 → update dist[Jules] = 10
- To Uri: 2 → 8 + 2 = 10 → update dist[Uri] = 10
- To Quill: 7 → 8 + 7 = 15 → update dist[Quill] = 15

Queue: (10, Jules), (10, Uri), (15, Quill)

---

**Step 3**: Process Jules (dist = 10)

From Jules:
- To Quill: 5 → 10 + 5 = 15 → Quill already has 15 → no update
- To Tess: already visited
- To Uri: 5 → 10 + 5 = 15 → Uri already has 10 → no update
- To Vale: 7 → 10 + 7 = 17 → update dist
