We are given an undirected weighted graph with the following nodes and edges:

**Nodes**: Cedar, Cleo, Lark, Nell, Poe, Quill, Sage, Tess, York  
**Edges and weights**:

- Cedar–Cleo: 8  
- Cedar–Lark: 5  
- Cedar–Nell: 6  
- Cedar–Poe: 3  
- Cedar–Sage: 2  
- Cedar–Tess: 4  
- Cleo–Lark: 8  
- Cleo–York: 3  
- Lark–Nell: 1  
- Nell–Tess: 6  
- Nell–York: 2  
- Quill–Sage: 9  

We are to find the **shortest path from Quill to Tess** in terms of total weight.

---

### Step 1: Understand the structure

Quill is only directly connected to **Sage** (weight 9).  
Tess is connected to:  
- Cedar (4)  
- Nell (6)

So, to get from Quill to Tess, we must go through Sage (only direct connection), and then from Sage to some node that connects to Tess.

Sage is connected to:
- Cedar (2)
- Quill (9)

So from Sage, we can go to Cedar (weight 2), and from Cedar we can go to Tess (weight 4).  
So one possible path:
**Quill → Sage → Cedar → Tess**  
Total weight: 9 + 2 + 4 = 15

Are there any other paths?

Let’s explore alternatives.

Can we go from Sage to other nodes?  
Sage is only connected to Cedar and Quill. So no other direct edges.

So from Sage, only options are:
- Quill (back)
- Cedar (weight 2)

So from Cedar, we can go to:
- Cleo (8)
- Lark (5)
- Nell (6)
- Poe (3)
- Tess (4)

So from Cedar → Tess is direct (4), which is better than going through others.

Can we go from Sage → Cedar → Lark → Nell → Tess?  
Path: Quill → Sage → Cedar → Lark → Nell → Tess  
Weights:  
9 (Quill→Sage) + 2 (Sage→Cedar) + 5 (Cedar→Lark) + 1 (Lark→Nell) + 6 (Nell→Tess) =  
9 + 2 + 5 + 1 + 6 = 23 → worse than 15

Cedar → Cleo → York → Nell → Tess?  
Cedar → Cleo: 8  
Cleo → York: 3  
York → Nell: 2  
Nell → Tess: 6  
Total: 8+3+2+6 = 19 → plus 9+2 = 21 → total = 9+2+8+3+2+6 = 30 → worse

Cedar → Poe? (3), but Poe has no connections to others. So dead end.

Cedar → Nell → Tess: 6 → so from Cedar to Tess via Nell is 6, but Cedar to Tess directly is 4 → better.

So best from Cedar to Tess is direct: 4.

So best path from Quill to Tess is:

**Quill → Sage → Cedar → Tess**  
Total: 9 + 2 + 4 = 15
